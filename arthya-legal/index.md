Arthya is a personal money ledger that runs on your phone. It does not have accounts, cloud sync or advertising, and it does not move money.

## What Arthya handles, and where it stays
- **Your ledger:** accounts, balances, buckets, transactions, split records and notes you enter or confirm. Stored only on your device.
- **Bank text messages (optional):** if you grant SMS permission, Arthya reads financial messages from banks to suggest ledger entries. It ignores one-time passwords, personal conversations and promotions. Messages recognised as financial transactions are kept on your device with their parsed details so you can trace every entry; while you set up, messages found in your chosen history range wait in a local draft until you approve or discard them. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
- **Notifications (optional):** Arthya can show local alerts such as "Transaction detected". Amounts are hidden on a secured lock screen.
- **Biometrics / screen lock:** used only to confirm your identity on your device. Arthya never receives your fingerprint or face data.
- **Backups you create:** a backup file is made only when you ask, is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and goes only where you save it. We cannot read or recover it.

## What leaves your device
Only one thing: when converted currency displays are refreshed, Arthya asks a public exchange-rate service (frankfurter.dev) for the rate between two currency codes (for example INR and USD). It does not receive your ledger, messages, name or device identifiers. Like any internet request, that service and your network provider can see normal connection details such as your IP address.

Arthya contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and does not sell or share personal data.

## Permissions
| Permission | Why | If you refuse |
|---|---|---|
| Read / receive SMS | Suggest ledger entries from bank messages | Add entries manually; everything else works |
| Notifications | Local alerts for detected transactions and bill reminders | No alerts |
| Biometric | Confirm identity for sensitive actions on your device | Use screen lock instead |
| Internet | Public exchange-rate lookup only | Converted displays are unavailable; the ledger works offline |

## Your choices
- Turn off SMS listening, notifications or auto-add at any time in the app or Android settings.
- Delete all data by clearing Arthya's storage or uninstalling it. We hold no copy.
- Because we hold no data about you, we cannot access, correct or delete it on your behalf; you control it directly on your device.

## Children
Arthya is not directed at children under 18.
