The short version

  • Your money data stays on your phone. No account, no cloud sync, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Bank messages are read on your device, only if you allow it. One-time passwords, personal chats and promotions are ignored.
  • One network request: a public exchange rate between two currency codes. Nothing about you or your ledger is sent.

1About this policy

Alloca is a personal money ledger that runs on your phone. It does not have accounts, cloud sync or advertising, and it does not move money.

This policy covers the version of Alloca published on Google Play. “We” means the developer named above.

2What Alloca handles

  • Your ledger: accounts, balances, buckets, transactions, split records and notes you enter or confirm. Stored only on your device.
  • Bank text messages (optional): read only if you grant SMS permission. See section 3.
  • Notifications (optional): Alloca can show local alerts such as “Transaction detected”. Amounts are hidden on a secured lock screen.
  • Biometrics / screen lock: used only to confirm your identity on your device. Alloca never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Backups you create: a backup file is made only when you ask, is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and goes only where you save it. We cannot read or recover it.

3Bank text messages

If you grant SMS permission, Alloca reads financial messages from banks to suggest ledger entries. It ignores one-time passwords, personal conversations and promotions.

Messages recognised as financial transactions are kept on your device with their parsed details so you can trace every entry. While you set up, messages found in your chosen history range wait in a local draft until you approve or discard them.

Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Bank messages are processed only on your device.

4What leaves your device

Only one thing: when converted currency displays are refreshed, Alloca asks a public exchange-rate service (frankfurter.dev) for the rate between two currency codes (for example INR and USD). It does not receive your ledger, messages, name or device identifiers. Like any internet request, that service and your network provider can see normal connection details such as your IP address.

Alloca contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and does not sell or share personal data.

6Security and storage

  • Your ledger is stored in Alloca’s private app storage on your device.
  • Alloca is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Backups you export are encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

7Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Delete all data by clearing Alloca’s storage or uninstalling it. We hold no copy.

8Your choices

  • Turn off SMS listening, notifications or auto-add at any time in the app or Android settings.
  • Delete all data by clearing Alloca’s storage or uninstalling it.
  • Because we hold no data about you, we cannot access, correct or delete it on your behalf; you control it directly on your device.

9Children

Alloca is not directed at children under 18.
